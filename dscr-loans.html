DSCR Loan vs Conventional Loan

When financing an investment property, you are often stuck comparing a DSCR loan vs conventional loan. Conventional loans require extensive DTI checks, tax returns, and personal income verification. A DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) loan ignores your personal income entirely. Approval is based strictly on whether the property's rental income covers the monthly mortgage payment.

🏢 No Personal Income Used

We do not verify your W-2s, tax returns, or employment. If the property cash-flows, you get approved.

♾️ Unlimited Properties

Conventional loans cap you at 10 financed properties. DSCR loans allow you to scale infinitely with no portfolio cap.

💼 Close in an LLC

Unlike conventional mortgages, DSCR loans allow you to close in the name of an LLC or Corporation to protect your personal liability. Keeping your property in layered LLCs is possible when we have consistent ownership.

📊 Easy Qualification

While many programs look for the Gross Rent to cover the Mortgage Payment (PITIA), we also work with specialized investors who do not have a minimum DSCR requirement at all, giving you ultimate flexibility.
