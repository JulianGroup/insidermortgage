First-Time Home Buyers

Navigate the home buying process with confidence. We provide a step-by-step roadmap and low-down-payment options.

Your Path to Homeownership

Buying your first home is incredibly exciting, but the mortgage process can feel overwhelming. We specialize in educating first-time buyers, breaking down the numbers, and finding the perfect loan program to fit your budget and long-term goals. We do not just process your loan; we provide a complete educational roadmap.

💰 Low Down Payment Options

You don't need 20% down. We offer Conventional loans with as little as 3% down and FHA loans starting at 3.5% down to help you keep cash in your pocket.

🤝 Down Payment Assistance

Depending on your income and location, we can connect you with local down payment assistance programs and grants to cover your upfront costs.

📊 Educational Approach

We record custom video breakdowns of your loan estimates so you understand exactly where every dollar is going. No hidden fees, no surprises.

🏡 Future Planning

We help you plan for the future, showing you how and when you can refinance later to drop your Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) and lower your payments.
