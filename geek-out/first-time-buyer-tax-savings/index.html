Transparency & Tax Modeling

Factors & Methodology Used in This Calculator

1. Housing & Loan Outflow Factors (Gross PITIA)

  • Loan Amount & Front-Loaded Amortized Interest: Standard fixed-rate mortgages are front-loaded with interest. This calculator sums exact month-by-month interest across your selected ownership window (12 months for a full calendar year, or your remaining months after closing).
  • Property Taxes & Local Assessments: Calculated as Purchase Price × Annual Property Tax Rate (defaulting to typical county averages by state: CA 1.20%, WA 0.95%, UT 0.55%, AZ 0.60%, FL 0.95%, fully editable for Mello-Roos or local assessments).
  • HOA Dues & Homeowners Insurance: Included in Gross Monthly PITIA for accurate cash-flow budgeting, and treated as non-deductible personal housing expenses.
  • 2026 PMI Deductibility: Private Mortgage Insurance (for down payments under 20%) is deductible under 2026 rules for borrowers with AGI under $100,000, phasing out linearly by 10% per $1,000 (or fraction thereof) between $100,000 and $110,000.

2. 2026 Income Tax Savings Factors (Incremental Benefit Over Renting)

  • 2026 Federal Standard Deduction & Progressive Brackets: Compares your renter baseline against 2026 Standard Deductions ($16,100 Single, $24,150 Head of Household, $32,200 Married Filing Jointly) so only the incremental tax benefit over renting is counted.
  • $750,000 Federal Mortgage Interest Cap & $40,400 SALT Cap: Federal mortgage interest is prorated up to $750,000 in acquisition debt. State and local taxes (state income tax + property tax) are capped at $40,400 for MAGI under $505,000, phasing down progressively by 30% above $505,000 until reaching a $10,000 floor at approximately $606,333 MAGI.
  • Prior-Year W-2 Box 1, Box 17 & 2026 Charitable Rules: Box 1 wages reflect taxable pay after pre-tax 401(k)/HSA deductions and are used as an estimate for AGI/MAGI (Married Filing Separately is not modeled). Box 17 reports state wage withholding and serves as an editable starting estimate for state income tax paid when computing your SALT baseline. Under 2026 Federal charitable rules, non-itemizers can deduct up to $1,000 ($2,000 Married Filing Jointly) above the line on top of the Standard Deduction, while Schedule A itemizers are subject to a 0.5% of AGI floor on charitable contributions.
  • State-Specific 2026 Rules: CA runs separately from the Federal SALT cap, using progressive 1%–12.3% brackets (+1% over $1M) with exact Single, HOH, and MFJ schedules, $5,900/$11,800 standard deduction, and a $1M mortgage interest cap (subject to California itemizing rules). UT uses a 4.45% flat tax with its 6% Taxpayer Tax Credit (subject to income phase-out). AZ uses a 2.50% flat tax conforming to federal deductions (excluding state income tax) with a $10,000 state SALT cap. WA & FL have 0% state wage income tax (optional federal state sales-tax elections are excluded to keep estimates conservative).
