How the U.S. Prime Rate Impacts Homeowners

HELOCs Move Dollar-for-Dollar with Prime

Virtually all traditional and credit union Home Equity Lines of Credit calculate your variable rate as Prime Rate + Margin. Whenever the FOMC raises or cuts the Federal Funds target range by 0.25%, the U.S. Prime Rate moves by the exact same 0.25%—adjusting most active HELOCs within one to two billing cycles.

30-Year Fixed Mortgages Track Bonds, Not Prime

Contrary to popular headlines, 30-year fixed mortgage rates do not move automatically with FOMC votes. Fixed mortgages follow long-term Mortgage-Backed Securities (MBS) & 10-Year Treasury yields, which often price in expected Fed moves weeks ahead of time.
