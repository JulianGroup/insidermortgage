California Proposition 19 Benefit

Keep Your Low Property Taxes When Moving

California homeowners age 55 and older can transfer their low Prop 13 tax base to a new home anywhere in California—even when buying a higher-value replacement home.

1 Current Property Assessment

Under Proposition 19, your transferred tax base starts from your current net assessed value on the tax roll, not the market price.
