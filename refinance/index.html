Understanding the Process

What is a Refinance?

Refinancing replaces your current mortgage with a new one. It pays off your existing debt and creates a new loan with new terms, potentially saving you thousands in interest or providing cash for large expenses.

The Step-by-Step Guide

  • Determine your financial goal (lower payment, cash-out, term change).
  • Check your home equity and credit score.
  • Gather income and asset documentation.
  • Lock your new interest rate and close!
