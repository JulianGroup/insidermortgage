Pro-Forma Term Index & Formula Definitions
∑ Tap any number below to see its step-by-step calculation
Effective Gross Income (EGI): Annual Gross Scheduled Rent across all units minus the 3.0% vacancy allowance. Represents actual collected rental income.
Operating Expenses (OpEx): Annual Property Taxes (1.25%, escalating +2%/yr), Home Insurance (0.35%, escalating +5%/yr), Monthly Maintenance Budget ( $200/mo), Property Management Fee (5% of EGI), and HOA dues. Excludes mortgage payments.
Net Operating Income (NOI): EGI − Operating Expenses. Measures the property's pure unlevered operating income before debt service.
Annual Debt Service:Total annual principal & interest (P&I) mortgage payments (Monthly P&I × 12). Subtracting Annual Debt Service from NOI yields Annual Net Cash Flow.
